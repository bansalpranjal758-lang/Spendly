Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect basic information such as your name, email address, and password. As you use the Service, we also collect the expense records you enter — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions — along with basic usage data like login timestamps that help us keep your account secure.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information you provide to operate the Service: authenticating your account, storing and displaying your expenses, and generating the summaries and insights you see in your dashboard. We do not use your financial data to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in Spendly's database and retained for as long as your account remains active. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data from unauthorized access. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting support.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or financial data with third-party advertisers. We may rely on trusted infrastructure providers (such as hosting services) to operate the Service, and those providers only process data as needed to keep Spendly running — they do not have independent rights to use your data.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, or if you would like to request access to or deletion of your data, please reach out to our support team through the contact details provided in your account settings.
